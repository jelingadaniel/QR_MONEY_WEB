# QR Money Website

A responsive, single-page website for presenting QR Money and its alignment with:
- Legal, Regulatory & Financial Access (SDGs 8, 10 and 16)
- Local Experience & Professional Networks (SDGs 8, 10 and 17)

## Run locally
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the `qr_money_website` folder.
3. Double-click `index.html` to open it in a browser.

For a local development server, open a terminal in this folder and run:
`python -m http.server 8080`

Then visit `http://localhost:8080`.

## Before publishing
- Replace `hello@qrmoney.example` with your real contact email.
- Update any product claims to match features that are actually implemented and approved.
- Add your real privacy policy, terms, and payment-provider disclosures.
- The QR illustration and wallet balance are demonstrations only. This site does not process payments.
