How it works

A simpler way to move money

QR Money aims to make everyday payments easier to initiate, track and confirm — especially for people and businesses that need accessible digital payment options.

▦

1. Create a payment QR

A payer or merchant prepares a QR payment request with the intended recipient and amount.

⌕

2. Scan and review

The payer scans the code, checks the payment details and confirms the transaction securely.

✓

3. Verify the result

The system checks the payment response before showing a transaction as completed.

The steps describe the intended product flow. Live payment processing depends on a configured and approved payment provider integration.

Legal, regulatory & financial access

Help people participate in the formal economy.

Access to work and income can be blocked by paperwork, unclear requirements and difficulty receiving payments. QR Money can serve as a practical payment layer alongside trusted guidance and referrals to the right support services.

1
Understand the requirements

Provide clear signposts to official work-authorization, business-registration and employer-compliance information.

2
Get ready to receive payments

Explain available payment channels, account requirements, transaction records and safe payment practices.

3
Keep a clear payment trail

Give users access to transaction history and payment confirmations to support their own record-keeping.
